# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Being patient with newcomers and less experienced members
- Providing constructive feedback
- Acknowledging mistakes and learning from them

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
- Harassment of any kind, including but not limited to discrimination based on protected characteristics
- Intimidation, threats, or aggressive behavior
- Spam or repetitive unwanted messages
- Deliberate exclusion or marginalization of people

## Enforcement

### Reporting Issues

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it by contacting a project maintainer:

- **Email** - rutova2@gmail.com (mark subject as "Code of Conduct Report")
- **Private Report** - Use GitHub's private reporting feature on this repository

**All reports will be:**
- Treated confidentially
- Investigated thoroughly
- Handled with care and respect for all parties

### Enforcement Response

Project maintainers will:

1. **Acknowledge** - Respond within 48 hours
2. **Investigate** - Gather information and context
3. **Consult** - Seek clarification if needed
4. **Decide** - Determine appropriate action
5. **Communicate** - Inform involved parties of outcome

Temporary or permanent bans may be issued for serious or repeated violations.

### Consequences

The consequences for unacceptable behavior depend on the severity and nature:

- **Warning** - First offense; private discussion and education
- **Temporary Suspension** - Repeated behavior; loss of participation rights
- **Permanent Ban** - Severe violations; removal from community

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces. Examples of representing a project include using an official project email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

This includes:

- Project repositories (GitHub)
- Issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Code reviews and feedback
- Documentation and examples
- Email communications
- Social media representations
- Project events and conferences
- Any other community interactions

## Project Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers will:

- Enforce this Code of Conduct
- Address violations promptly and fairly
- Keep the decision process transparent
- Create a safe and inclusive environment
- Set an example through their own behavior
- Respond to reports confidentially

## Community Member Responsibilities

Community members are expected to:

- Understand and follow this Code of Conduct
- Report violations to maintainers
- Be respectful in disagreements
- Support a positive and inclusive culture
- Welcome diverse perspectives
