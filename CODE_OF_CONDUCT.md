# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Blazor Component Library community is dedicated to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We commit to:

- **Creating a safe environment** where people of all backgrounds feel welcome
- **Treating everyone with respect** regardless of experience level
- **Focusing on constructive collaboration** and learning
- **Maintaining professional and inclusive communication**

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- **Be respectful** - Value different perspectives and approaches
- **Be professional** - Keep discussions focused and productive
- **Be inclusive** - Welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels
- **Be helpful** - Assist others and share knowledge generously
- **Give credit** - Acknowledge contributions and original ideas
- **Follow the rules** - Respect community guidelines and decisions

## Examples of Positive Behavior

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being patient with newcomers and less experienced members
- Providing constructive feedback
- Acknowledging mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Celebrating successes and achievements

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

- **Harassment** - Including unwanted attention, insults, or attacks
- **Discrimination** - Based on race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, or other protected characteristics
- **Offensive content** - Including slurs, harassment, or sexually explicit material
- **Intimidation** - Threats, bullying, or aggressive behavior
- **Spam** - Repetitive or unwanted messages
- **Exclusion** - Deliberately excluding or marginalizing people

## Enforcement

### Reporting Issues

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it:

1. **Directly to the person** - If you feel safe, address it calmly and respectfully
2. **To a maintainer** - Contact the project maintainer privately
3. **Email** - vz@sarmkadan.com (mark as "Code of Conduct")

**All reports will be:**
- Treated confidentially
- Investigated thoroughly
- Handled with care

### Response Process

When a report is received:

1. **Acknowledge** - We will respond within 48 hours
2. **Investigate** - We will gather information and context
3. **Consult** - We may ask for clarification
4. **Decide** - We will determine appropriate action
5. **Communicate** - Both parties will be informed of the outcome

### Consequences

Depending on the severity and nature of the behavior:

- **Warning** - First offense; discussion and education
- **Temporary Suspension** - Repeated behavior; loss of access
- **Permanent Ban** - Severe or ongoing violations; removal from community

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues and pull requests
- Discussions and comments
- Code reviews and feedback
- Documentation and examples
- All community interactions

## Responsibilities of Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Enforcing this Code of Conduct
- Addressing violations fairly
- Being transparent about decisions
- Creating a safe, inclusive environment
- Setting an example through their own behavior

## Responsibilities of Community Members

Community members are responsible for:

- Reading and understanding this Code of Conduct
- Following these guidelines
- Reporting violations
- Being respectful in disagreements
- Supporting a positive culture

## Disagreement & Conflict Resolution

We value diverse perspectives. However:

- **Disagree respectfully** - Focus on ideas, not people
- **Assume good intent** - Most people mean well
- **Listen actively** - Try to understand other viewpoints
- **Find common ground** - Look for areas of agreement
- **Escalate if needed** - Involve moderators if necessary

## Questions or Feedback

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct or suggestions for improvement:

- **Open an issue** - Public discussion for feedback
- **Email maintainer** - Private conversation preferred
- **Discuss** - Engage respectfully with the community

## Amendments

This Code of Conduct may be updated. Changes will be:

- Announced in advance
- Discussed with the community
- Documented with rationale
- Effective immediately upon announcement
